# Bright-Car-Sales-Case-Study

1. Problem Statement

Bright Motors recently appointed a new Head of Sales to improve dealership performance, optimize inventory, and increase overall profitability. However, the company needed deeper insights into historical vehicle sales data to better understand customer purchasing trends, top-performing car models, regional sales performance, and pricing patterns.

The challenge was to analyze a large transactional dataset and transform the raw data into meaningful business insights that can support future sales and marketing strategies.


2. Aim of the Project

The aim of this project was to analyze the Bright Car Sales dataset to identify sales trends, revenue drivers, customer preferences, and regional performance in order to support data-driven business decisions at Bright Motors.


3. Objectives

To achieve the project aim, the following steps were taken:

* Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis
* Analyzed total revenue and sales performance across vehicle makes and models
* Investigated the relationship between vehicle price, mileage, and year of manufacture
* Identified regions with the highest and lowest sales volumes
* Explored customer purchasing trends and preferences
* Created visual dashboards and charts to present findings clearly
* Developed recommendations to improve dealership profitability and operational efficiency


4. Summary of Results

The analysis produced the following key findings:

* Total revenue generated: 7.61 Billion
* Total units analyzed: 290,000 vehicles
* Low mileage vehicles showed stronger sales performance and higher customer demand
* Certain regions significantly underperformed compared to others
* Some vehicle models consistently generated lower sales and revenue

Recommendations

* Prioritize stocking and marketing low mileage vehicles
* Implement strategies to improve performance in underperforming regions
* Review and reassess poor-performing vehicle models
* Use customer purchasing trends to guide future inventory and marketing decisions


5. Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preliminary analysis
* Databricks – SQL queries and large-scale data analysis
* Canva – Presentation and dashboard design
