# Bright-Car-Sales-Case-Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes historical vehicle sales data for **Bright Motors**, a car dealership aiming to improve sales performance, optimize inventory, and increase overall profitability.

The project focuses on using data analysis to uncover insights into **vehicle sales, revenue performance, customer preferences, pricing, mileage, vehicle age, and regional sales trends**.

The goal was to transform a large transactional dataset into meaningful business insights that can help Bright Motors make more informed decisions around inventory, sales strategies, marketing, and dealership performance.

---

## Problem Statement

Bright Motors recently appointed a new Head of Sales to improve dealership performance. However, the company needed a better understanding of its historical vehicle sales data to identify important sales and customer trends.

The challenge was to analyze the transactional dataset and determine which vehicle makes and models perform best, which regions generate the most sales, and how factors such as **price, mileage, and year of manufacture** influence purchasing patterns.

The insights from this analysis can help Bright Motors identify areas of strong performance as well as opportunities for improvement.

---

## Aim of the Project

The aim of this project was to analyze the **Bright Car Sales dataset** to identify key sales trends, revenue drivers, customer preferences, and regional performance in order to support **data-driven decision-making** at Bright Motors.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of the project were to:

* Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
* Analyze total revenue and sales performance across vehicle makes and models.
* Identify the best and worst-performing vehicles.
* Investigate the relationship between vehicle price, mileage, and year of manufacture.
* Identify regions with the highest and lowest sales volumes.
* Explore customer purchasing trends and preferences.
* Analyze factors that may influence vehicle demand.
* Create visualizations and dashboards to communicate the findings.
* Develop recommendations to improve sales performance and profitability.

---

## Analysis Process

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was reviewed and prepared to ensure that the data was suitable for analysis. This included checking the data structure, identifying potential inconsistencies, and preparing relevant fields for further analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

The dataset was analyzed to understand overall sales performance and identify important trends across:

* Vehicle makes and models
* Revenue
* Units sold
* Vehicle prices
* Mileage
* Year of manufacture
* Regional performance

### 3. Sales & Revenue Analysis

Sales and revenue were compared across different vehicle makes and models to identify the strongest revenue drivers and products that may require further investigation.

### 4. Regional Analysis

Sales performance was analyzed across different regions to identify high-performing and underperforming areas.

### 5. Customer & Vehicle Analysis

The analysis explored purchasing patterns and the relationship between factors such as **vehicle mileage, price, and age** to better understand customer preferences.

## Key Results

The analysis produced several important findings:

* **Total revenue generated:** 7.61 Billion
* **Total units analyzed:** 290,000 vehicles
* Low-mileage vehicles showed stronger sales performance and higher customer demand.
* Certain regions significantly underperformed compared to others.
* Some vehicle models consistently generated lower sales and revenue.
* Vehicle characteristics such as mileage, price, and age can provide useful insights into customer purchasing behaviour.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Top 15 Makes by Revenue

Ford leads with **$259.9M**, almost double Chevrolet at **$136.6M**. Ford, Chevrolet and Nissan together make up **37.2% of total revenue**.

### 2. Top 15 Models by Revenue

The **F-150** is the top model at **$49.0M**, followed by **Altima ($39.7M)** and **Escape ($33.0M)**.

### 3. Sales by Region

**FL (15,501)** and **CA (14,746)** record the most sales. The top five states — **FL, CA, TX, GA and PA** — account for **50.7% of all sales**.

### 4. Sales by Manufacturing Year

Vehicles from **2012** have the most sales at **18,237**. Model years **2012 to 2015** together make up **52.4% of sales**.

### 5. Average Selling Price by Year

Average selling price rises with newer vehicles. Average prices increase from **$581 for 1990 models** to **$16,397 for 2012 models** and **$26,196 for 2015 models**.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend

**February 2015** recorded the highest monthly sales with **35,345 sales**, followed by **January 2015 with 30,514 sales**. April 2015 (**310**) and July 2015 (**190**) recorded the lowest sales among the main months.

### 7. Top 10 Body Types

**Sedan (44,622)** and **SUV (25,567)** are the most sold body types, together accounting for **70.2% of all sales**.

### 8. Mileage vs Selling Price

Selling prices are highest for low-mileage vehicles and fall as mileage increases. Vehicles with **under 20,000 miles** average **$21,038**, while vehicles with **over 150,000 miles** average **$3,318**.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations were developed:

### 1. Prioritize Low-Mileage Vehicles

Bright Motors should consider increasing the availability and marketing of low-mileage vehicles due to their stronger sales performance and customer demand.

### 2. Improve Underperforming Regions

The dealership should investigate why certain regions are performing below average and consider targeted marketing campaigns, promotions, or changes to inventory.

### 3. Review Poor-Performing Models

Vehicle models with consistently low sales should be reviewed to determine whether pricing, demand, availability, or marketing strategies need to be adjusted.

### 4. Use Data to Guide Inventory Decisions

Historical sales trends should be used to determine which vehicles should receive greater inventory allocation and which models should be stocked more cautiously.

### 5. Improve Marketing Strategies

Customer purchasing trends can be used to create more targeted marketing campaigns based on vehicle preferences, pricing, and regional demand.
